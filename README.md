# Teichmüller Coordinates for Nondegenerate Polygonable One-Holed Dilation Torus

**Fulin Zhong — September 2026**

[**Read the current manuscript (PDF)**](./Teichmuller%20space%20of%20One%20holed%20dilation%20torus.pdf)

We study the marked Teichmüller space of nondegenerate polygonable dilation structures on the one-holed torus.

## Main theorem

Let \(S\) be a one-holed torus with a marked boundary point, and let \(\mathcal T_{\mathrm{poly}}^\dagger(S)\) denote the marked Teichmüller space of polygonable dilation structures with nontrivial linear holonomy and nonzero boundary translation.

The enhanced holonomy map

$$
\widehat{\mathrm{Hol}}:\mathcal T_{\mathrm{poly}}^\dagger(S)\longrightarrow \mathcal E^\dagger
$$

is a connected regular \(\mathbb Z\)-covering. Moreover,

$$
\mathcal E^\dagger \cong \mathcal B\times(\mathbb R^2\setminus\{0\}),
\qquad
\mathcal B\cong \mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb R),
$$

and this covering is precisely the universal covering in the second factor. Consequently,

$$
\boxed{\mathcal T_{\mathrm{poly}}^\dagger(S)\cong \mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb R)\times\mathbb R^2.}
$$

More precisely, there is a real-analytic diffeomorphism

$$
\Psi:\mathcal T_{\mathrm{poly}}^\dagger(S)\xrightarrow{\sim}\mathcal B\times\mathbb R^2
$$

such that the enhanced holonomy map becomes

$$
\operatorname{id}_{\mathcal B}\times\varpi,
\qquad
\varpi(s,\theta)=e^s(\cos\theta,\sin\theta).
$$

Thus the deck group is \(\mathbb Z\), acting by

$$
(\beta,s,\theta)\longmapsto(\beta,s,\theta+2\pi k),
\qquad k\in\mathbb Z.
$$

## Structure of the argument

The manuscript develops explicit pentagonal room coordinates and identifies the nondegenerate enhanced holonomy space. It then studies the relative mapping class group action and labelled cut systems, proves that the enhanced holonomy fibres are exactly indexed by the central \(\mathbb Z\)-family, and establishes the global covering theorem. Finally, angular monodromy identifies this covering with the universal cover of the punctured logarithmic-holonomy factor, giving the global Teichmüller coordinates above.

The appendix contains the positive-basis forest and the rational-root analysis, including the strict and weak walks used in the fibre classification.
